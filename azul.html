What we cook

Saúl came to New York to cook Italian food, and he has spent his career putting Mexican flavor and European technique on the same plate. Azul cooks whatever your event calls for.

  • MexicanMole, birria & live taco bars.
  • ItalianPasta & Italian classics.
  • BrunchEggs Benedict & brunch favorites.
  • Plant BasedFresh, vibrant & full of flavor.
  • SweetsA sweet finish to every celebration.
  • Off the GrillRib eye & charred vegetables.
  • CanapésElegant bites for every celebration.

How it works

  1. Tell us

    Build your menu right here on the page, or just tell us the date, the place and how many people.

  2. Saúl writes the menu

    He sends a menu for your event, and we adjust it with you until it’s right.

  3. We cook it there

    We bring everything, cook on site and serve it hot.

The chef

Saúl Montiel grew up cooking in his family’s fonda in Hidalgo, Mexico, and built his career in New York kitchens. He is the chef and owner of La Única Tacos & Tequila in Elizabeth, New Jersey, and you’ve seen him on TODAY, Chopped, Epicurious and Telemundo.
